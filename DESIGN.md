# Design System: Moni yem

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Violet Wallet"**

Moni yem is the category standard for a personal finance app, executed without irony: one solid violet card holding the money, everything else calm, tinted, and quiet around it. The quality bar is Revolut, Wave, Monarch / Copilot Money and Apple Wallet. The home screen answers one question first ("where am I this month?") and refuses the gadget dashboard full of widgets. Density is iOS-native: generous 14 to 18px panel padding, 42px category tiles, 48px buttons, a thumb-height tab bar with the add action in the middle.

The world is built from violet-tinted neutrals (every grey carries hue 315 to 320), a single saturated brand color, and a fixed 12-color category palette that the user picks from. Depth is soft and ambient, tinted with the brand's own dark plum. Motion borrows iOS physics: a drawer curve for sheets, pickers and notifications, a quick ease-out for presses. Native browser pickers are replaced by in-world ones (calendar, time wheels). Light and dark are both first-class and the user can switch at any time from the header.

All money is tabular, integer FCFA with comma grouping and the currency as a smaller, dimmed suffix (`3,012,250 FCFA`). The interface copy is English, second person, short and warm ("Good evening,", "2 days without an entry", "Time for a quick check-in?").

**Key Characteristics:**
- One solid violet balance card (#9518ad) per screen at most; violet elsewhere is sparse (active tab, FAB, primary button, switches, selected day, focus).
- Violet-tinted neutrals and plum-tinted shadows.
- Every category color comes from one 12-token palette, each with a dark-mode value, and is tinted at 14%.
- Manrope, tabular figures everywhere, tight negative tracking on large numbers.
- Generous, nested rounded corners (28 / 26 / 22 / 16 / 14 / 12 / 10px), tile radius scaled to tile size.
- Lucide line icons at 1.75 stroke, 20px default.
- Custom calendar and time-wheel pickers: bottom sheet on mobile, anchored popover on desktop.
- Light, dark and system themes, with a visible header toggle.

## Colors

A tinted-plum neutral field carrying a single saturated violet, with semantic green/red and a 12-color category palette.

### Primary
- **Moni yem Violet** (brand): the balance card fill, FAB, primary buttons, active switches, the selected calendar day, chart income bars and savings line, focus outlines, text selection, caret, notification dot. Identical in both themes; the brand constraint from PRODUCT.md.
- **Pressed Violet** (brand-press / brand-press-dark): hover and pressed fill for violet buttons and the FAB. Darker in light mode, lighter in dark mode.
- **Violet Ink** (brand-ink / brand-ink-dark): violet used as text: "See all" links, active tab label, active rail item, today in the calendar, "Soon" badge, "Edit" actions. Never use raw brand as small text on light surfaces; use this.
- **Lilac Wash** (brand-soft, brand-soft-2, and dark counterparts): the avatar, active rail item, selected chip, a past-date pill in the add sheet, reminder settings tile, new-row flash.

### Secondary (category palette)
Twelve fixed hues, each with a lighter dark-mode value: **Violet**, **Purple**, **Blue**, **Cyan**, **Teal**, **Green**, **Amber**, **Orange**, **Coral**, **Red**, **Pink**, **Slate** (pal-* tokens). Every spending, income and savings category stores a palette key, never a raw color. The category hue is the icon color on a `color-mix(in oklab, <hue> 14%, transparent)` tint (tiles, chips, the selected icon option). In savings it also colors the stacked bar segment, legend swatch and goal progress fill.

Defaults: Contributions amber, Bank blue, Family coral; Groceries green, Transport cyan, Housing purple, Bills slate, Health red, Eating out pink, Family & gifts orange; Salary teal, Side business blue, Other income amber. New categories start on violet.

### Tertiary (semantic)
- **Ledger Green** (pos / pos-dark): income amounts, positive deltas (on a 12% tint pill), the sync tile and dot. Spending amounts are not red; they stay ink with a "−" sign.
- **Alert Red** (neg / neg-dark): field errors ("You already have a spending category called “Groceries”.") and the destructive "Delete category" action. Nothing else.
- **Toast Accents** (toast-ok, toast-link and dark counterparts): the check icon and "Undo" link on the inverted toast. They flip per theme because the toast inverts.
- **Reminder Amber** (warn-bg / warn-ink): the reminder banner only; its small button inverts the pair.

### Neutral
- **Lavender Mist** (bg / bg-dark): the page.
- **Card White / Night Plum** (surface / surface-dark): panels, groups, settings lists, sheets, pickers.
- **Soft Lilac Grey** (surface-2 / surface-2-dark): segmented tracks, ghost buttons, inputs, icon options, value buttons, the time-wheel band, hover fill, progress tracks.
- **Hairline** (line, line-strong): row dividers, tab bar top border, danger-zone rule, inactive switch, sheet grabber, the dashed "New" tile ring, spending bars in charts.
- **Plum Ink** (ink, ink-2, ink-3 and dark counterparts): primary text, secondary text, tertiary meta (dates, captions, placeholders, weekday letters).

### Named Rules
**The One Violet Card Rule.** Solid brand fill on a large surface is reserved for the balance card. Every other surface is white/night plum on the tinted page; violet appears in small doses.

**The Tinted Neutral Rule.** Every neutral, border and shadow carries the violet hue (around oklch hue 315 to 320). Do not introduce neutral greys or black shadows in light mode.

**The Palette Key Rule.** A category's color is always one of the 12 pal-* tokens, chosen by key, with its own dark value and a 14% tint. No category gets a raw hex.

**The Quiet Spending Rule.** Spending is shown in ink with a "−" sign, not red. Only income is colored (green). Red means something is wrong or destructive.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Manrope (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI)
**Body Font:** Manrope
**Label/Mono Font:** none; figures use Manrope's tabular numerals

**Character:** One geometric-humanist family across every role, loaded as a 400 to 800 variable axis and used at intermediate weights (450 to 750). Hierarchy comes from size, weight and tracking, never a second family.

### Hierarchy
- **Display amount** (650, clamp(44px, 13vw, 56px), 1.05, -0.04em): the live amount in the add sheet.
- **Display balance** (650, clamp(34px, 9.5vw, 44px), 1.1, -0.035em): net worth on the balance card.
- **Display pot / savings** (650, clamp(32px, 9vw, 40px) and clamp(30px, 8vw, 38px), 1.1): the pot detail balance and the savings total.
- **Headline** (650, 22px mobile / 28px desktop, 1.15, -0.02em): screen titles ("Activity", "Savings", "Categories", "Edit category").
- **Picker date** (700, 26px mobile / 22px desktop, -0.025em): the selected date in the calendar header ("Fri, Oct 17").
- **Picker wheel** (560, 23px, tabular; 720 for the selected item and the colon): hour and minute wheels.
- **Subtotal** (650, 20px, -0.02em): card-level figures ("Left this month") and the pot name; the home savings total uses 24px at the same weight.
- **Title large** (650, 18px): the category name in the editor preview. The money input value also sits at 18px/600.
- **Title** (650, 17px, -0.015em): section headings ("My savings", "Icon", "Color") and sheet titles ("New transaction").
- **Title small** (620 to 650, 16px): savings group names and totals.
- **Input** (16px): every text input on mobile (search, note, editor fields), so iOS does not zoom on focus.
- **Body** (400, 15px, 1.45): default text; row titles at 540 to 560; amounts at 600.
- **Body small** (14px, 1.35): links, card labels, settings values, field labels, notification body, value buttons.
- **Label** (13px): secondary lines, day headers (600), segmented and chip labels (560).
- **Caption** (12px, 550): meta under amounts, weekday letters (650), flow labels on the card ("Income · Oct").
- **Tab label** (11px, 550): tab bar and badges (650).

The currency suffix is 0.62em of the amount, weight 500, 72% opacity (0.42em, 60% in the add sheet).

### Named Rules
**The Tabular Money Rule.** Every number is set with tabular figures, slight negative tracking (-0.01em), no wrapping, comma thousands grouping and a dimmed "FCFA" suffix: `125,000 FCFA`. Calendar days and time wheels are tabular too.

**The Single Family Rule.** Manrope only. Contrast through weight steps and size, not through a display face.

**The No-Zoom Input Rule.** Text inputs are at least 16px on mobile.

## Layout

Mobile first. The main column has 16px side padding, top padding of the safe-area inset plus 12px, and bottom padding of 104px plus the safe area so content clears the tab bar. Panels stack with 14px gaps; section headers sit 26px above a panel and 10px below. Rows bleed to the panel edge with a negative margin so hover and press fills reach the edge. A destructive zone sits 40px below the last action, behind a hairline.

- **720px and up:** page padding 28px 32px; savings groups go to two columns; the icon grid goes from 6 to 9 columns and the swatch row from 6 to 12.
- **1024px and up:** a 248px left rail replaces the tab bar (brand mark, primary "Add" button, nav, dark mode toggle, sync status). Home becomes two columns (1.35fr / 1fr, 22px gap); savings groups go to three columns; Activity is capped at 780px, Settings and the category manager at 720px, Starting balances at 620px; content max width 1180px. The add sheet becomes a centered 440px dialog; pickers become 336px popovers anchored to their trigger.
- **Below 420px:** balance-card flow icons drop out; below 340px the flows stack.

## Elevation & Depth

A soft, layered system. Surfaces sit on the tinted page with a near-invisible shadow; lifted elements (sheets, pickers, toast, notification) take a deep, diffuse plum shadow. Shadows are always vertically offset and heavily blurred, never hard. In dark mode the tint is dropped for black at higher opacity. Glass appears only on chrome that floats over content (tab bar, notification preview).

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Resting** (`0 1px 2px rgba(45,10,55,.06), 0 1px 1px rgba(45,10,55,.04)`): panels, groups, settings lists, icon buttons, search, segmented thumb.
- **Raised** (`0 6px 18px -6px rgba(45,10,55,.14), 0 2px 6px -2px rgba(45,10,55,.06)`): available for lifted cards.
- **Overlay** (`0 24px 48px -16px rgba(45,10,55,.28), 0 8px 16px -8px rgba(45,10,55,.12)`): add sheet, pickers, toast, notification.
- **Balance card** (`0 14px 28px -14px rgba(30,8,36,.45), 0 2px 6px -2px rgba(30,8,36,.2)`) and **FAB** (`0 8px 16px -8px rgba(30,8,36,.5), 0 2px 4px rgba(30,8,36,.18)`): violet objects get their own deeper plum drop.
- **Selection rings** are drawn with spread-only shadows, not elevation: a 2px surface gap plus 2px category ring on the sheet's category tile, a 3px gap plus 2px ring on the selected swatch, a 2px inset ring on the selected icon option.
- **Glass**: tab bar at 88% surface with `saturate(1.6) blur(18px)`; notification at 82% surface with `blur(24px) saturate(1.8)`.
- **Scrims**: plum `rgba(20,6,26,.42)` behind the add sheet, `.34` behind mobile pickers, none behind desktop popovers.

### Named Rules
**The Plum Shadow Rule.** Light-mode shadows are tinted rgba(45,10,55) or rgba(30,8,36), never neutral black, and never hard-edged.

## Shapes

Generous, nested continuous-feeling rounds, getting smaller as elements nest: sheets and mobile pickers 28px (top corners), balance card and pot header 26px, panels, groups and desktop popovers 22px, FAB 18px (a rounded square, not a circle), flow cells 16px, rows, buttons, keys, icon options and inputs 14px, segmented tracks, fields and the wheel band 12px, small buttons 10px, segmented thumbs and value buttons 9px. Focus outlines are 2px with an 8px radius.

Category tiles scale their radius with their size, at about 30%: 56px / 17px (editor preview), 44px / 14px (sheet picker), 42px / 13px (rows), 40px / 13px (group headers), 36px / 11px (home breakdown), 34px and 32px / 10px (settings, fields). The app icon follows the same ratio (40px / 11px). Calendar days are 44px cells at 13px.

Pills (999px) are reserved for chips, mini-chips, badges, the month switcher, deltas and the switch; icon buttons, calendar arrows, swatches and the avatar are circles. Bars and tracks use half their height (12px bar / 4px, 8px progress / 4px, 6px track / 3px, 4px mini track / 2px), swatches 2 to 3px. Dividers are 1px hairlines inside lists; the first row has none.

The balance card carries a subtle native ornament: a soft white radial glow bottom-right and concentric hairline rings top-right at 2 to 14% white. This is the card's material, not a reusable pattern.

## Components

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep solid violet (#9518ad) on large surfaces to the balance card; use violet ink for violet text.
- **Do** tint neutrals and light-mode shadows with the plum hue.
- **Do** set every amount with tabular figures, comma grouping and a dimmed "FCFA" suffix (`3,012,250 FCFA`).
- **Do** give every category a pal-* key and render it as the hue on a 14% tint, in both themes.
- **Do** ship every new surface in light and dark, and keep the header theme toggle reachable.
- **Do** nest radii from 28px down to 9px as elements nest, scale tile radius with tile size, and keep pills for chips, switches and badges.
- **Do** use the in-world calendar and time-wheel pickers instead of native date and time inputs; sheet on mobile, anchored 336px popover on desktop.
- **Do** keep text inputs at 16px or more on mobile.
- **Do** isolate destructive actions in a danger zone and explain why they are disabled.
- **Do** use Lucide line icons at 1.75 stroke (2.1 to 2.2 for active and FAB states).
- **Do** open sheets, pickers and banners on the drawer curve and close them faster.

### Don't:
- **Don't** color spending red; use ink with "−" and keep red for errors and destructive actions.
- **Don't** give a category a raw hex outside the 12-color palette.
- **Don't** add a second typeface or a display font; contrast comes from Manrope weights.
- **Don't** use hard, unblurred offset shadows or neutral black shadows in light mode.
- **Don't** fill the home screen with widgets; it answers "where am I this month?" first.
- **Don't** add uppercase labels above headings; uppercase lives only inside the OS-style notification header.
